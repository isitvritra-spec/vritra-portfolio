Fair points — EF in the hands of an expert can produce good SQL. but most projects don’t have EF experts and nobody wanna waste the whole life in learning just a tool... that is replacable... . They have devs writing LINQ + Includes, and suddenly the ORM generates a monster query nobody expected.

With Dapper, there’s no guessing — the SQL is exactly what you wrote. If I want a join, I write it. If I only need one table, I keep it simple. EF adds a layer of abstraction that can work, but it also hides too much until you hit performance walls. THAT'S TOTALLY WHAT U WORKIN ON....
