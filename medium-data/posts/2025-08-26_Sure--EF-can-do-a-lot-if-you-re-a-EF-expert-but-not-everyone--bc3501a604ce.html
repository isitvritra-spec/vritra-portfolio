Sure, EF can do a lot if you’re a EF expert but not everyone. In real projects, 90% of teams aren’t EF experts. Missed includes, hidden joins, N+1 traps, unexpected memory spikes — all of these silently eat time and money.

Dapper isn’t perfect either, but at least what you write is exactly what hits the database. In business, predictability, performance, and maintainability matter far more than having a perfect ORM skillset on every dev! That’s the reality most articles don’t mention... that's not my fault!
